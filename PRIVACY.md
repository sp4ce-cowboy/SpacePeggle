# Comet Drop: privacy policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

Comet Drop is the App Store release of SpacePeggle, an iPad game by
[sp4ce-cowboy](https://github.com/sp4ce-cowboy).

**Comet Drop doesn't collect any data.**

- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The game doesn't connect to the internet.
- Levels you design are saved only on your iPad, in the app's own folder. They leave your
  device only if you move them yourself, and deleting the app deletes them.
- The app contains no third-party code or services.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first.

Questions: open an issue at
[github.com/sp4ce-cowboy/SpacePeggle/issues](https://github.com/sp4ce-cowboy/SpacePeggle/issues).
