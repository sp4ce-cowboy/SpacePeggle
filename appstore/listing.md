## Description [4000]

Comet Drop is a physics arcade game for iPad. Aim the cannon, fire a comet and watch it
ricochet through a field of pegs. Clear every orange peg before you run out of balls.

FEATURES
• Three built-in levels, and as many of your own as you like.
• Real physics: every bounce, collision and explosion is simulated, frame by frame.
• Special pegs: Kaboom pegs explode and push everything nearby; Spooky pegs turn the floor
  into a portal back to the top; Stubborn pegs can be knocked into other pegs.
• Pegs with hit points, and solid blocks to play around.
• A moving bucket: land the comet in it for a big bonus.
• Four planets with their own gravity: Space, Mars, Saturn and Earth. Switch them mid-game.
• A trajectory guide that bends with gravity, so you can line up your shot.
• Level designer: place, rotate and resize pegs, set their hit points, then save your level
  and play it.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. Works offline.

Comet Drop started as a university project at the National University of Singapore and was
polished for release. Its physics engine is written from scratch, and the source code is on
GitHub.
